# Privacy Policy – Personal Data Firewall

Last updated: January 2026

## Overview
Personal Data Firewall is a browser extension designed to help users understand how personal data may be entered or transmitted while browsing the web. The extension operates entirely on the user’s device.

## Data Collection
Personal Data Firewall does not collect, transmit, or store personally identifiable information.

The extension may locally observe user activity, such as form input events or network request metadata, for the sole purpose of detecting potential personal data patterns (for example, email addresses or phone numbers). Any stored information is limited to timestamps, website domains, and redacted samples.

## Data Storage
All data is stored locally on the user’s device. No data is sent to external servers.

## Data Sharing
Personal Data Firewall does not sell, share, or transfer user data to third parties.

## Tracking and Advertising
The extension does not include advertisements and does not perform user tracking.

## Legal Disclaimer
Personal Data Firewall is an educational and experimental tool. It does not provide legal advice and does not guarantee compliance with privacy regulations such as GDPR or CCPA.
